## 3. Five stories that matter

### 3.1 [Diesel margins surge to highest on record as supply tightens](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/diesel-margins-surge-to-highest-on-record-as-supply-tightens) - Bloomberg, 1 Sep 2026
- What happened: US diesel crack above $106/bbl. Hormuz disruption since late February, Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian refineries and a Russian fuel-export ban; US distillate stocks at record seasonal lows on heavy exports.
- Why it matters for a trader: the east-of-Suez distillate pull keeps product tankers bid and the Atlantic diesel arb open. Distillate-versus-gasoline is the crowded trade; the risk is a demand break, not more supply.
- Talking point: "The diesel crack is doing the work that flat price used to do. What does your desk think breaks it first, demand or a Hormuz reopening?"

### 3.2 [Copper falls to one-month low](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/copper) - Trading Economics, 2 Sep 2026
- What happened: COMEX copper down to ~$6.42/lb as higher oil and bond yields revived rate fears, while the Middle East escalation prolongs a sulphuric-acid shortage for SX-EW producers. [MINING.com](https://www.mining.com/copper-price-holds-near-record-as-london-warehouse-bidding-war-looms/) reports an LME warehouse bidding war looming.
- Why it matters for a trader: macro sells futures while physical tightens. That is a premium and spread trade, not an outright: watch CME-LME arb, physical premia and nearby LME spreads as warehouses compete for metal.
- Talking point: "Copper is down on macro but acid supply is hitting SX-EW: is the desk seeing that in cathode premiums yet?"

### 3.3 [EU gas at highest since January 2023 as Hormuz fears return](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/eu-natural-gas) - Trading Economics, 2 Sep 2026
- What happened: TTF up 29% in four weeks to ~EUR 74/MWh. EU storage was ~62% full on 20 Aug, the lowest for the date in over five years ([S&P Global, 17 Aug](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/electric-power/081726-eu-gas-storage-passes-60-full-but-still-lags-recent-years-gie)); QatarEnergy's force majeure was extended in July ([Bloomberg, 28 Jul](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/qatarenergy-extends-lng-force-majeure-for-european-buyers)).
- Why it matters for a trader: Europe must outbid Asia for US cargoes; TTF-JKM, Atlantic LNG freight and the winter-summer TTF spread are where the risk is priced. Coal-to-gas switching reverses, which is bullish Newcastle.
- Talking point: "With a $21 TTF-Henry Hub spread, shipping is the constraint, not molecules. Is the desk long freight?"

### 3.4 [Russia pauses grain export duty through end-2026 as Black Sea shipping stalls](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-02/russia-pauses-grain-export-duty-through-the-end-of-2026) - Bloomberg, 2 Sep 2026
- What happened: Ukrainian attacks have stalled Black and Azov Sea shipments that carry over 70% of Russian grain exports; Ukraine's deep-water ports are seen shut until December or January ([Grain Central, 1 Sep](https://www.graincentral.com/markets/daily-market-wire-1-september-2026/)). - Why it matters for a trader: Russian FOB offers get cheaper on paper but cannot ship, so origin switches to EU, US and Australia. Bullish CBOT and Matif spreads, bearish Black Sea freight utilisation, congestion at Constanta and on the Danube.
- Talking point: "Cheaper Russian wheat that can't load is a freight and origination problem: who picks up the Egypt and Algeria tenders?"
