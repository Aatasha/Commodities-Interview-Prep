## 1. Glencore in 60 seconds

- **What it is:** Baar-based producer and marketer of 60+ commodities, listed in London (GLEN) and Johannesburg. Two segments: **Marketing** (the trading business, reported as Adjusted EBIT) and **Industrial** (mines and plants, Adjusted EBITDA). "Over 140,000 employees and contractors" in 30+ countries. Sources: [2026 Half-Year Report, 5 Aug 2026](https://www.glencore.com/media-and-insights/news/2026-half-year-report), [2025 Annual Report](https://www.glencore.com/media-and-insights/news/publication-of-2025-annual-report).
- **People:** CEO Gary Nagle (since July 2021), CFO Steven Kalmin (since 2005). Jyothish George has run metals, iron ore and coal trading since Aug 2025. Sources: [Glencore leadership](https://www.glencore.com/who-we-are/our-leadership), [MINING.com, Aug 2025](https://www.mining.com/web/glencore-shakes-up-trading-team-with-head-of-coal-to-exit/).
- **Produces:** copper, cobalt, zinc, nickel, thermal coal, steelmaking coal (Elk Valley, Canada), ferroalloys. **Markets:** all of those plus oil and products, iron ore, aluminium and alumina; agri exposure now through a ~16% stake in Bunge after Viterra merged into Bunge on 2 Jul 2025. Sources: [Half-Year Production Report, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.glencore.com/media-and-insights/news/half-year-production-report-2026), [AJ Bell, Jul 2025](https://www.ajbell.co.uk/news/articles/glencore-closes-viterra-bunge-merger-launch-1-billion-buyback).

## 3. Five live themes and a talking point for each

1. **Marketing is on for a record year.** H1 EBIT of $3.3bn is already near the top of the annual range. *Ask:* "Which desks drove H1, and is volatility normalising into H2?" [Bloomberg, 29 Jul 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-29/glencore-says-its-traders-post-first-half-profit-of-3-3-billion)
2. **Radiant World: a $480m counterparty hit.** On 29 Aug 2026 Glencore provisioned its entire net exposure to Singapore iron-ore trader Radiant World amid allegations of falsified documents to banks; Nagle said new business was halted and remaining contracts are being exited. *Ask:* "How do counterparty limits and document checks change after something like Radiant?" [Bloomberg, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-29/glencore-took-480-million-provision-on-radiant-world-exposure). Do not lead with this; use it only if trading risk comes up.
3. **Copper, going it alone after Rio.** Merger talks with Rio Tinto collapsed in Jan 2026 (third failed attempt). Targets: >1Mt/yr by end-2028, ~1.6Mt by 2035, Alumbrera restart Q4 2026, plus the Collahuasi-Quebrada Blanca conveyor negotiation with Anglo Teck (~$1.4bn/yr EBITDA prize). *Ask:* "How does the trading book position around the group's own copper growth?" [Rio Tinto statement, Jan 2026](https://www.riotinto.com/en/news/releases/2026/no-intention-to-bid-statement), [MINING.com, Aug 2026](https://www.mining.com/glencore-stands-between-anglo-teck-and-1-4b-copper-prize/)
4. **Cobalt quotas.** DRC replaced its export ban with quotas through at least 2027; Glencore's 2026 allocation is 22,800t (KCC 16,100t, Mutanda 6,700t), above-quota tonnes are stockpiled in-country, and Glencore was the first miner to export under the regime. *Ask:* "How do you price and hedge stockpiled units you cannot yet ship?" [Kitco/Reuters, 30 Apr 2026](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-04-30/glencore-expects-drc-cobalt-exports-normalize-line-2026-quotas)
5. **Coal: kept, declining, and having a moment.** The Board retained coal after consulting shareholders, with a managed run-down of thermal mines to the mid-2040s, yet Newcastle coal hit an 11-week high on LNG shortages. *Ask:* "Does the coal book's cash still fund the copper build-out, and how do you think about that trade-off?" [Glencore retention decision](https://www.glencore.com/media-and-insights/news/retention-of-the-coal-and-carbon-steel-materials-business-), [Trading Economics coal, 1 Sep 2026](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/coal)

Other recent items if they come up: Viterra-Bunge closed Jul 2025; Li-Cycle recycling assets bought out of bankruptcy Aug 2025; London primary listing retained Aug 2025 after studying New York; US DOJ monitorships from the 2022 bribery settlements ended early in Mar 2025 ([Compliance Week](https://www.complianceweek.com/anti-bribery/glencore-compliance-monitorships-ended-early-by-doj/35938.article)).

## 6. Ten questions to ask traders

1. What does a normal Tuesday look like on your desk, hour by hour?
2. How much of your P&L comes from logistics and optionality versus a flat-price view?
3. How has the desk's use of time spreads changed with the current curve shape?
4. What is the hardest part of the physical-to-paper handoff, and where do juniors get it wrong?
5. How do you size a position you are only 60% confident in?
6. What surprised you most in your first year on the desk?
7. What does a bad day look like, and how does the desk debrief it?
8. Which data do you actually look at first in the morning?
9. How do the trading and industrial sides talk to each other on copper or coal?
10. If you were starting again this year, which desk would you want to rotate through first?

## 9. Avoid

- Reciting prices without a view. Every number you mention needs a "because".
- Asking about pay, bonuses or hours.
- Raising the 2022 settlements yourself. If a trader raises them: acknowledge, note the monitorships ended early in 2025, move on.
- Monopolising one trader. Ten minutes, thank them, move.
- Drinking more than one. Traders remember.
- Forgetting names. Note them in your phone immediately after each conversation.
